# FAKE DOCUMENT RED-FLAG CHECKLIST
## What AI-Generated Fakes Get Wrong

**How to use this checklist:** Run every rental application through it before you score it. A single red flag is a reason to verify harder; two or more means slow down and verify everything independently. Nothing here replaces an official background or identity check — this is the human layer AI fakes still fail at.

**Tone principle:** Calm, methodical, adversarial — you are checking *your own* documents before you trust them. Never accuse an applicant of fraud based on this list alone. Flags trigger verification, not rejection. General information only — not legal advice.

---

## 1. PAY STUBS — What AI Fakes Get Wrong

AI-generated pay stubs look convincing at a glance and break on the second look. Check these, in order:

**a) Math that doesn't foot.** Multiply the pay-period gross by the number of pay periods in the year. It must equal the YTD gross. AI fakes routinely ship YTD figures that don't equal any multiple of the period amount. Do the multiplication yourself — don't eyeball it.

**b) Missing YTD logic.** Real stubs show YTD for gross pay, federal tax, state tax, and deductions, and each YTD must be *larger* than the current-period figure and *plausible* for the claimed start date. A stub claiming 11 months of employment with a YTD equal to 3 pay periods is fiction.

**c) Deduction nonsense.** Federal withholding must be roughly proportional to gross across periods; FICA should be ~7.65% of gross (Social Security + Medicare) up to the wage cap. AI fakes often invent deduction amounts that don't scale with the gross or repeat the identical tax figure across wildly different gross amounts.

**d) Font and layout inconsistencies.** Zoom to 200%. Real payroll output uses one font family at consistent sizes. Look for: mismatched fonts between the company header and the numbers table, irregular letter spacing, misaligned columns, blurry logos next to razor-sharp text (a classic AI tell), and dollar signs that sit at different heights.

**e) Employer detail that dead-ends.** The employer's EIN, address, and phone must exist. AI generators invent plausible-sounding companies ("Summit Logistics Group") at addresses that don't exist on any map. Google the address independently.

**Quick rule:** If any two of (a)–(e) fail, treat the stub as unverified and go to the employer-callback script in `03-verification-workflow.md`.

## 2. BANK STATEMENTS — What AI Fakes Get Wrong

**a) Suspiciously rounded numbers.** Real statements are full of ugly numbers — $1,247.83, not $1,200.00. AI fakes love round figures for deposits, rent payments, and the closing balance. A statement where most transactions end in .00 was not produced by a real bank.

**b) No micro-transactions.** Real life has $3.50 coffees, $12.99 subscriptions, $47.22 gas station stops. AI-generated statements skew toward a few large, clean transactions and omit the noise of daily spending. No small transactions over a 60-day window is a flag.

**c) Balance math that doesn't chain.** Opening balance + deposits − withdrawals − fees must equal the closing balance, for *every* statement period, and the closing balance of month 1 must equal the opening balance of month 2. AI fakes break the chain — check all three months.

**d) Deposits that don't look like payroll.** A salary claimed at $6,000/month should appear as a repeating deposit (direct deposit labeled "PAYROLL" or similar) roughly every 2 weeks or monthly, not as irregular cash deposits of varying amounts. Irregular large deposits are income the applicant can't document.

**e) Missing bank DNA.** Real statements carry the bank's exact formatting, footer disclosures, page numbers ("Page 1 of 4"), and consistent fonts. AI fakes get the logo right and the boilerplate wrong — compare against a real statement from the same bank if you can.

**What to request:** 3 full months of statements, all pages, unredacted except you may allow the account number to be masked to the last 4 digits. Refusal to provide them is itself a flag.

## 3. IDs — Hologram and Edge Cues You Can Check by Photo

You can't feel a hologram through a photo, but you can catch fakes anyway:

**a) Ask for two photos at different angles.** Have the applicant photograph the ID straight-on and tilted ~30 degrees. A real ID's hologram, ghost image, and optically variable ink shift color and visibility between the two shots. If both photos look identical in every reflective area, the "hologram" was printed flat.

**b) Edge and laminate cues.** Zoom in on the edges: real IDs have clean, uniform card edges and laminate that meets the edge evenly. Look for: visible cut lines, laminate that peels or bubbles at corners, thickness inconsistencies, or a photo area that looks pasted on (sharp rectangle boundary around the portrait).

**c) Microprint and guilloche.** Real IDs have fine-line security patterns (guilloche) and microprint that degrades gracefully when zoomed. AI fakes render these as mush — the fine lines blur into gray smears at 300% zoom instead of resolving into crisp patterns.

**d) Data consistency.** Name, date of birth, ID number, issue and expiry dates must be internally consistent (expiry typically 4–8 years after issue depending on jurisdiction — verify locally) and must match the application exactly. One digit off anywhere is a stop-everything flag.

**e) The photo test.** Compare the ID photo to a live video call or a fresh selfie. AI-generated faces on fake IDs often have subtly wrong ears, glasses frames that merge into skin, or hairlines that don't match a real selfie. A 30-second video call defeats most ID fakes.

## 4. THE 2-MINUTE SNIFF TEST

Do this on every application before scoring. Set a timer — two minutes, no more:

| Step | Time | Action |
|------|------|--------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:20 | **Foot the pay stub.** Period gross × periods-per-year = YTD gross? Yes/no. |
| 2 | 0:20–0:45 | **Chain the statements.** Month 1 close = month 2 open? Rounded-number scan — mostly .00? |
| 3 | 0:45–1:10 | **Zoom the ID.** Two angles requested; hologram shifts? Edges clean? Data matches the application? |
| 4 | 1:10–1:35 | **Search the employer.** Does the company address exist on a map? Phone number real? |
| 5 | 1:35–2:00 | **Count your flags.** 0 → proceed to score. 1 → verify that document independently. 2+ → full verification workflow before any decision. |

**Golden rules:**
1. Flags trigger verification, never accusation. Ask for the *real* document or an independent confirmation; don't confront.
2. Verify the two highest-impact documents (income proof, identity) on every application — fraud concentrates there.
3. Document what you checked and when. If a tenancy goes wrong, your verification notes are your defense.
4. This checklist is general information only — not legal advice. Document-handling and adverse-action rules vary by jurisdiction: verify locally.
